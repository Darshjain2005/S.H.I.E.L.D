2
ADNEXUS
THE
S.H.I.E.L.D
Develop an Agentic AI cybersecurity assistant that ingests and normalizes security logs, detects and correlates suspicious activities through hybrid ML and rule-based analysis, and uses collaborating AI agents to investigate threats, explain evidence, recommend response actions, and generate incident reports.
Intelligent Log Ingestion & Normalization:
Ingests heterogeneous security logs from endpoints, servers and network sources, transforming raw events into a standardized format for unified analysis.
Hybrid Threat Detection Engine:
Combines deterministic security rules with ML-based anomaly detection to identify both known threats and previously unseen suspicious behaviour.
Multi-Agent Investigation Core:
➢ 
Log Analysis Agent:
 Extracts suspicious events, identifies indicators and summarizes relevant evidence.
➢ 
Threat Investigation Agent:
 Correlates related events across users, hosts, IPs and timelines to reconstruct potential attack chains.
➢ 
Response Recommendation Agent:
 Determines appropriate containment and remediation actions based on threat severity and evidence.
Automated SOC Intelligence:
Generates risk-scored incidents with evidence-backed explanations, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, recommended actions and downloadable incident reports through a real-time dashboard.
Collaborative Agentic SOC:
Unlike conventional SIEM/IDS solutions that primarily generate alerts, ADNEXUS uses specialized AI agents that 
collaborate to investigate and reason over security incidents.
Multi-Layer Threat Intelligence:
Fuses 
rules + anomaly detection + behavioural analysis + event correlation
 to improve detection accuracy while reducing alert fatigue.
Evidence-Grounded AI Investigation:
Every AI conclusion is tied to actual security events, timestamps, entities and detection evidence, reducing unsupported AI-generated conclusions.
Attack-Chain Correlation:
Builds relationships between seemingly isolated events to identify 
multi-stage attacks, lateral movement and coordinated malicious behaviour.
Agent-in-the-Loop Response:
AI recommends containment and remediation actions while keeping the 
final decision with the security analyst
, ensuring safe and controlled incident response.
